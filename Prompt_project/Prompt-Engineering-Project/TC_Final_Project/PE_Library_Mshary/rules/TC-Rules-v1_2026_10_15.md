|---|---|---|

| Fairness | Before approving a new version, the owner compares equivalent formal and dialect inquiries for differences in facts, tone and next steps. | Was a comparison reviewed? |

| Human decision | Before sending any reply or making any eligibility, approval or complaint decision, an authorized employee reviews and decides; AI drafts only. | Did a person approve the decision? |
