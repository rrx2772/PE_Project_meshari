# Library Guide — Training Center (B1)

The library contains one approved-policy Facts block, five reusable templates and five responsible-use rules adapted from the supplied Day 4 library. The templates are drafts requiring staff review.

## Usage
1. Open the required template from `templates/`; copy the full template text.
2. Replace `[FACTS]` with the current approved text from `blocks/` and provide `[INPUT_TEXT]` as invented or pre-masked text. Fill `[LANGUAGE]`, `[TONE]`, `[MAX_WORDS]` or use their declared defaults.
3. Keep fixed RULES unchanged. Never place real names, contact details, IDs or bank details in an AI prompt. For T5, perform initial local masking before sending any data externally; use only synthetic data for model tests.
4. Review the generated main output and the internal CHECK. Remove CHECK before sending a reply to a learner.
5. Score Test 1 (RACE 5 points), Test 2 (template 6 points; two different fills and one edge case), and Test 3 (output 5 points). Do not approve unless all scores are full. Verify factual support, exact dates/numbers, no promises, no sensitive data, and natural completeness.
6. Record a screenshot from an actual approved test, a masked sample input, scores and reviewer decision in the report.

## Ownership
Library owner maintains templates and versions. Staff reviewer verifies case facts and approves drafts. Authorized employee decides all eligibility or status outcomes. The AI makes no decisions and has no access to live records.

## Known limits
Historical screenshots in chat are not automatically mapped to the final versions. Until screenshots and full scoring are verified, this package is a review-ready draft, not evidence of full approval.
